Row 66 · 2026-09-27 · REAL-21 · §73 Facts versus skill

Row 68 · 2026-09-27 · MODEL-9 · §72 Encoder confidence objectives

Which training loss gives a small encoder trustworthy confidence?

Why we ran it

Row 53 built a small encoder categoriser: ModernBERT-large with one scored [MASK] slot per category, following Laya's layout. For auto-filing, its confidence has to be trustworthy. The owner noted that Jev, a published decision model, trains with a loss that accounts for confidence.

What we tried

The row 53 encoder (1,500 steps, no record), on all four folds of REAL-6 and POI-1, with four losses:

  • cross-entropy (the baseline);
  • label smoothing 0.1;
  • log score plus spherical score (the pair behind Laya's RL training, here with its exact gradient);
  • cross-entropy plus Brier score.

Each was read by ECE (expected calibration error: the gap between stated confidence and actual accuracy), AURC (mean error across all coverage levels; lower is better) and coverage at 98%. Soft targets from a teacher and multiple-choice pre-training were planned but not run.

query, 24 shots as "statement -> category" [SEP]

Row 71 · 2026-09-26 · MODEL-12 · §65 Hop limits in reasoning

Hop limits: how many links of a chain can a model follow?

Why we ran it

The owner asked (2026-09-26): given "a = b, b = c, …", how many hops can a model follow in one pass? Encoders and decoders, short and long names. Can "thinking" steps, looping layers or diffusion denoising raise the limit? Categorising is itself a short chain: query, similar example, its label. The encoder came from row 53.

What we tried

An item set of four shuffled chains of k links (k = 1 to 12) with six options, so chance is 17%. v1 leaked the answer through its options; a control caught this, and v2 fixed it. Readers:

  • untrained Qwen2.5 0.5B to 14B, one pass and with a chain of thought;
  • ModernBERT-large in row 53's layout trained on k ≤ 3 or ≤ 12, plus a looped version, labelled lines with a hop loss, and "scratch rounds" (each hop written back into the input);
  • Huginn (recurrent depth);
  • diffusion models LLaDA, Dream and LFM2.5.

Example prompt

Each line below says that two names are the same thing. Links chain together: if a = b and b = c, then a = c. Follow the links from the start name until the chain ends.

- dish = renewed
- favored = vivid
- collects = jumps
- renewed = controls
- controls = destroys
[... 7 more lines ...]

Start at dish. Where does the chain from dish end?

k = 3: dish → renewed → controls → destroys.

How it did

What we learned

  • One pass follows as many hops as it was trained on, plus one or two. Untrained decoders follow one.
  • Writing each hop back and re-reading removes the limit, as a chain of thought does.
  • Looping, labelling and a hop loss each add about one hop.
  • Untrained diffusion and recurrent models fail, except Dream-7B, whose denoising steps act like a chain of thought.
