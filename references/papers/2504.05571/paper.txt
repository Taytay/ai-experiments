For the CPT, Rephrase CPT, and Knowledge-Instruct experiments, we used a learning rate of of 1e - 05,
a batch size of 4, a context length of 2, 048. For the SFT phase of the CPT and Rephrase CPT training

Synthetic CPT experiments, we used the exact same code and configurations as detailed in the official
