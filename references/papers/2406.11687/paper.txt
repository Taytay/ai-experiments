tion between token lengths before and after introducing
typographical errors on MMLU and GSM8K, varying
n-gram settings (2, 3, or 5). The x-axis represents the

   Figure 11 shows a strong positive correlation be-
tween token lengths before and after introducing
typographical errors across different tasks (GSM8K
and MMLU) and n-gram settings (2, 3, 5). We ob-
serve that token lengths after introducing errors are
proportional to their original lengths. Besides, both
GSM8k and MMLU tasks exhibit similar patterns,
and the n-gram settings (2, 3, 5) do not significantly
alter this relationship. Most interestingly, the slope
for reorder errors is relatively larger than for noise
errors, indicating that reorder errors tend to re-
sult in a slightly greater increase in token length
compared to noise errors.
